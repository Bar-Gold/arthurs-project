# Facebook Auto-Poster — getting started

You only have to do this once. It takes about two minutes.

## 1. Open the app

Find **Facebook Auto-Poster** in the Start Menu and open it.

The first time it opens, it shows a **Welcome** screen with three things it
needs. It works through them one at a time and tells you which one it is on.

## 2. Let it start Chrome

The app posts through its own separate Chrome window, kept off your screen so
it never gets in your way. It is not the Chrome you browse in, and it will not
touch your normal browsing, bookmarks or logins.

If the Welcome screen asks, press **Start Chrome** and wait a few seconds.

> If it tells you Chrome is not installed, install Google Chrome from
> **google.com/chrome** first, then reopen the app.

## 3. Log into Facebook

Press **Log in to Facebook**. A Chrome window opens on your screen showing
Facebook.

Log in there exactly as you normally would. Tick "remember me" if it offers.

Then come back to the app and press **Check again**.

**The app never asks for your Facebook password and never logs in for you.**
You log in yourself, once, in a real browser window. The app just uses the
window afterwards.

When all three lines have a green tick, press **Start using the app**.

## Day to day

The sidebar is the order you work in:

1. **Compose** — write the post, attach pictures
2. **Groups** — tick which groups it goes to
3. **Publish** — post now, once at a set time, or on repeat

**Queue** below the line is where you check what happened.

## Things worth knowing

**The app has to be open for scheduled posts to go out.** If you close the
window, it will warn you when something is still waiting. If you asked for it
to start when you sign in, it will come back on its own after a restart.

**Posts go out slowly on purpose** — 10 to 25 minutes between groups. A batch
of six groups takes a couple of hours. That is deliberate: posting to many
groups quickly is what gets Facebook accounts restricted.

**Posts only go out between 08:00 and 23:00.** Anything due outside that waits
until morning.

**The same wording is never sent to a group twice.** If you set up a repeating
post, give it a few different wordings and it will rotate them.

**If a group holds your post for an admin to approve**, the app notices, says
so on the Queue screen, and checks back later on its own.

## Posting as a different Facebook account

Press **Switch account**, under the connection light on the left. It will ask
you to confirm, then open a Chrome window with the Facebook login in it — sign
in as the other account there and press **Check again**.

Your groups, templates, repeating posts and everything the app has already
posted stay exactly as they are. Only who it posts as changes. Two things are
worth knowing before you do it:

* You will need the other account's password. The app never has it and never
  asks for it.
* The new account has to be a member of the groups in your list, or posts to
  those groups will fail. Have a look at the Groups screen afterwards.

If a post is going out at that moment, the app will ask you to wait until it
has finished.

## If something looks wrong

Look at the coloured dot at the bottom of the sidebar.

| Dot says | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Connected** | Nothing. All good. |
| **Chrome not running** | Press the **Start Chrome** button under it. |
| **Logged out** | Press **Log in to Facebook** and sign in again. |
| **Checkpoint!** | Facebook wants to confirm it is you. Open the Chrome window and finish the check yourself. |

There is a **Check connection** button under the dot you can press any time.

### If you are asked to check your setup

In the Start Menu, under **Facebook Auto-Poster**, there is **Check my setup**.

Open it. It looks at this PC and shows a short list of what it can and cannot
find. Take a photo or a screenshot of that list and send it on — it says
exactly what is wrong, which saves a lot of guessing.

## Removing it

Uninstall from Settings → Apps like any other program. Your groups, templates,
posting history and Facebook login are left on the PC, so if you reinstall
later everything is still there.
