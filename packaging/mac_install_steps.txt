Installing Facebook Auto-Poster on your Mac
============================================

Your Mac will warn you the first time, because the app is not yet registered
with Apple. This is expected. It takes one extra step, once.

1. Double-click the file FacebookAutoPoster-Setup-....pkg.

2. Your Mac says it cannot be opened, or that Apple could not check it.
   Click "Done" (or "OK"). Do NOT click "Move to Bin".

3. Open System Settings (the grey gear in the Dock, or the Apple menu at the
   top left, then System Settings).

4. Click "Privacy & Security" in the list on the left, then scroll down to the
   "Security" part.

5. You will see a line about FacebookAutoPoster-Setup. Click "Open Anyway".
   Enter your Mac's password (or use Touch ID) when it asks.

6. The installer opens. Click Continue, read the policy, click "Agree", then
   Install. It asks for your Mac's password once more.

7. When it finishes, the app opens by itself. Next time, open it from
   Applications or from Launchpad.

Good to know
------------

- Google Chrome must be installed. The installer warns you if it is not.
- Closing the window with the red button keeps the app posting in the
  background; its icon stays in the Dock. Click the icon to bring it back.
- To quit the app: click its name at the top left of the screen, then "Quit",
  or right-click its icon in the Dock and choose "Quit".
