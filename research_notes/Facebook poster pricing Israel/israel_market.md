# Israeli market context for pricing a Facebook group posting tool for real-estate agents (ILS / month)

All pages below were accessed on 2026-11-03 unless a different date is given. "+VAT" means the vendor quotes the price before VAT (18%).

## 1. Israeli services and tools that post to Facebook groups: what they charge

### Takeaway
There are at least four Israeli (Hebrew-site) products that do exactly this job, and they sit between about 160 and 300 ILS per month + VAT for one account. PostPro, the one aimed squarely at real-estate agents (metavchim), charges 300 ILS/month + VAT; Nexus charges 249-289 ILS/month + VAT; PostPilot (a Windows desktop app, like ours) charges 160 ILS per profile per month + VAT for 1-4 profiles.

### Cited Findings
- **PostPro** (postpro.co.il): one plan, **300 ILS/month + VAT**, first week free (starts with the first post, no credit card), no commitment, no setup fee. Aimed at real-estate agents, recruiters, car dealers. Runs in the cloud ("a dedicated computer in the cloud"; the user's PC can be off). Up to 10 text variations per post, up to 7 posting rounds a day, support by WhatsApp and Google Meet, 20-minute onboarding call before the first post. Accessed 2026-11-03. [PostPro](https://postpro.co.il/)
- **PostPro's own blog** says automation pays off for an agent only when groups x listings x times-per-week passes roughly 100 posts a week; below that, manual posting (about 20 minutes a day for one listing in a few groups) is manageable. It says automation does not bring more leads, only saves effort. Article dated August 2026. [PostPro blog](https://postpro.co.il/blog/matai-pirsum-otomati-mishtalem.html)
- **Nexus** (nexus-data.co.il): **289 ILS/month + VAT** monthly with no commitment and standard support; **249 ILS/month + VAT** on a 12-month commitment with "preferred" technical support. Both: unlimited posts, "anti-block" algorithm, scheduling and repeating posts, templates, public group search, no download or browser extension. Accessed 2026-11-03. [Nexus](https://nexus-data.co.il/)
- **PostPilot** (postpilot.co.il): priced per Facebook profile per month, **before VAT**: 1-4 profiles 160 ILS each; 5-9 profiles 145 ILS (example 725 ILS); 10-14 profiles 130 ILS (1,300 ILS); 15-30 profiles 115 ILS (1,725 ILS); 31+ profiles 95 ILS (2,945 ILS). **Desktop app for Windows** by default, cloud on request. Free trial, no card, monthly or annual billing, cancel any time. Accessed 2026-11-03. [PostPilot](https://postpilot.co.il/)
- **FaceBoost on 4card.co.il**: Windows software, "monthly subscription" listed as 499 ILS (regular 650 ILS) plus a one-time 399 ILS setup fee; lifetime licence 3,999 ILS (regular 4,999 ILS). No trial, "all sales are final". The page wording mixes "monthly" and "annually" for the 499 ILS price, so the billing period is unclear. Does far more than group posting (auto-joining groups, friend requests, inbox messaging, Marketplace). Accessed 2026-11-03. [4card FaceBoost](https://www.4card.co.il/a-system-for-automatic-advertising-on-facebook/)
- **BrokerCRM**, an Israeli CRM for agents, has a "Facebook plan" at **504 ILS per active broker per month + VAT**, against 212 ILS for basic and 333 ILS with telephony. So the Facebook part is valued at about 170-290 ILS/month on top of the CRM. (The page does not spell out what "Facebook" covers.) Accessed 2026-11-03. [BrokerCRM](https://brokercrm.co.il/real-estate-brokerage-software)
- Other Israeli tools found with no visible price: Robo Postim [robopostim.com](https://www.robopostim.com/), fbzipper [fbzipper.com](https://fbzipper.com/he/home), FboomTool (its shop page said "no products to display") [fboomtool.com](https://www.fboomtool.com/fbrobot).
- General Facebook marketing (paid ads, not group posting): one Israeli guide says an ad campaign manager costs at least 2,500 ILS/month, and packages start from 1,197 ILS/month. These are for managing paid ads, a different service. [tzd-seo.org.il](https://tzd-seo.org.il/facebook-prices/); [alai.co.il](https://alai.co.il/facebook-sponsored-promotion/facebook-advertising-packages-pricing-guide/) (seen in search results only, not opened)

### Inferences
- The going rate in Israel for one agent's automated group posting is **about 250-300 ILS/month + VAT** (roughly 295-355 ILS including VAT). PostPilot's 160 ILS is the low end, for the same desktop model as ours.
- PostPro is the closest competitor (real-estate focus, WhatsApp support, Hebrew). Its 300 ILS + VAT, with a free week and no commitment, is the price a metavech will compare against.
- The cloud services (PostPro, Nexus) have one selling point our desktop app lacks: posting while the PC is off. PostPilot shows a desktop app can still sell, but it charges less.
- The market norm is no commitment, a free trial, and a cheaper price for a yearly commitment (Nexus: 40 ILS/month off).

### Gaps
- I found no Israeli service where a person posts to groups by hand for an agent (a "manual posting" freelancer) with a public monthly price. Searches for "שירות פרסום בקבוצות פייסבוק מחיר" brought up only automation tools and paid-ad agencies.
- Robo Postim and fbzipper prices were not on their pages.
- How many customers each competitor has, and whether these are their real prices or launch prices, is unknown.

## 2. What Israeli agents pay monthly for CRM / listing software and portals

### Takeaway
The only official per-agent CRM price found is BrokerCRM: 212-504 ILS per broker per month + VAT. BMBY, Madlan Pro and Yad2 do not publish prices. One 2026 article uses 2,000 ILS/month to Yad2 as an example of what an agent spends.

### Cited Findings
- **BrokerCRM**: 212 ILS (basic), 333 ILS (with telephony), 504 ILS (Facebook plan), per active broker per month, before VAT. Accessed 2026-11-03. [BrokerCRM](https://brokercrm.co.il/real-estate-brokerage-software)
- **BMBY**: no public price list; quotes are custom, on request. (From search results; BMBY's own site was not opened.) [BMBY](https://bmby.co.il/bmby-3d/); [digitalix CRM comparison 2026](https://www.digitalix.co.il/recommended-crm-systems-israel/)
- **Yad2**: no official agent package prices found. Yad2's terms forbid sharing one package between agents, with a 5,000 ILS fine (from a search summary of the terms page, not verified on the page). [Yad2 terms](https://my.yad2.co.il/Takanon.php)
- An article by AutoFlowr (dated 2026-06-29) uses **"2,000 ILS a month to Yad2"** for an agent who has kept it 4 years, and says Yad2 promotion prices rise 5-15% a year. This is the author's example, not an official price, and the author sells agent websites (a competitor to Yad2). [AutoFlowr](https://www.autoflowr.co.il/blog/real-estate-agent-website-vs-yad2)
- A Globes article reports that in Tel Aviv only 21% of flats-for-sale ads on the boards were private; the rest were agents (date of the article not checked). [Globes](https://www.globes.co.il/news/article.aspx?did=1001299805)
- **Madlan Pro** ("מדלן למקצוענים"): the page exists but returned HTTP 403, and no price was found. [Madlan proAgent](https://www.madlan.co.il/proAgent)
- **Nadlan One** (agent software): page returned HTTP 403, no price found. [Nadlan One](https://nadlanone.co.il/en/)

### Inferences
- An active agent's monthly tool bill is plausibly a CRM (about 200-500 ILS + VAT) plus portal ads (the one example: about 2,000 ILS). Next to that, 250-300 ILS for group posting is a small line, in the same range as a CRM seat.

### Gaps
- Official Yad2 agent package prices, Madlan Pro prices, BMBY prices, and Homie prices: not public or not reachable. Getting them needs a phone call or a sales quote.

## 3. Freelancer rates in Israel for social media management for small businesses

### Takeaway
A social media freelancer costs about 800-3,000 ILS/month in Israel (8-15 posts a month); agencies 2,500-7,000 ILS. A posting tool at 250-300 ILS is about a tenth of that.

### Cited Findings
- CK Digital 360 (2026): freelancer **800-3,000 ILS/month**, typically 8-15 posts a month with replies within 2-4 hours and a basic monthly report; agency **2,500-7,000 ILS/month** (20-30 posts plus stories and reels). "Professional management averages 1,500-7,000 ILS monthly." Does not say whether VAT is included. [CK Digital 360](https://ckdigital360.co.il/sushial-media/nihul-social-media-le-esek-israel-2026/)
- The same search found other 2026 Israeli price guides saying a good freelancer for Instagram + Facebook is 1,500-2,500 ILS/month, and a basic one-channel package of 8-12 posts is 1,500-3,000 ILS; and that automated social tools start from 109 ILS/month with no commitment. These came from search summaries only; the pages were not opened. [Brandy TLV](https://www.brandytlv.com/blog/social-media-management-cost-2026); [Lunsoul Social](https://lunsoulsocial.com/he/pricing); [lemel](https://lemel.co.il/social-media-management-price/)

### Inferences
- A social media manager makes content; our tool only distributes it. So the fair comparison is the hours saved (an hour or so a day posting by hand to 40-80 groups), not the freelancer fee. Still, the freelancer fee shows the ceiling of what a small business will pay for "Facebook done for me".

### Gaps
- No source on a freelancer who only does group posting for agents, priced per month.

## 4. Israeli VAT 2025-2026, quoting prices with or without VAT, osek patur vs osek murshe

### Takeaway
VAT is 18% since 1 January 2025, and the 2026 budget is built on 18%. Every Israeli B2B tool found quotes its price **before VAT** ("+ מע"מ"). An osek patur charges no VAT, up to a yearly turnover ceiling of 122,833 ILS in 2026.

### Cited Findings
- VAT rose from 17% to **18% on 1 January 2025**, per the Tax Authority announcement; invoices issued before 31.12.2024 but paid after 1.1.2025 need a supplementary invoice for the difference. [Deloitte Israel tax alert 19/2024](https://www2.deloitte.com/content/dam/Deloitte/il/Documents/tax/tax_alerts/2024/19.2024.pdf); [Efraty CPA guidance](https://www.efraty.com/%d7%9e%d7%99%d7%93%d7%a2-%d7%a9%d7%99%d7%9e%d7%95%d7%a9%d7%99/%d7%9e%d7%a2%d7%9e/%d7%94%d7%a0%d7%97%d7%99%d7%95%d7%aa-%d7%a2%d7%9c%d7%99%d7%99%d7%aa-%d7%9e%d7%a2%d7%9e-%d7%9c-18-%d7%9e%d7%99%d7%95%d7%9d-1-%d7%91%d7%99%d7%a0%d7%95%d7%90%d7%a8-2025/) (both from search results, not opened)
- The 2026 budget is built on 18% VAT. [Bizportal](https://www.bizportal.co.il/guides/news/article/20041450) (search result summary, not opened)
- Pricing convention: Nexus ("+VAT"), PostPro ("300 ILS + VAT"), PostPilot ("before VAT") and BrokerCRM ("before VAT") all quote B2B prices before VAT. [Nexus](https://nexus-data.co.il/); [PostPro](https://postpro.co.il/); [PostPilot](https://postpilot.co.il/); [BrokerCRM](https://brokercrm.co.il/real-estate-brokerage-software)
- Osek patur turnover ceiling: **120,000 ILS for 2025, 122,833 ILS for 2026**. An osek patur does not charge VAT; if they go over the ceiling they pay VAT on the difference and become an osek murshe (automatically once the annual report shows it). The advice is to switch ahead of time if you expect to go over. [Green Invoice (Morning)](https://www.greeninvoice.co.il/magazine/תקרת-עוסק-פטור/) (article first dated 2024-09-02, with 2025 and 2026 figures shown when accessed 2026-11-03)

### Inferences
- If the seller is an osek murshe, quote "X ILS + VAT"; the agent (almost always an osek murshe themselves, since they charge 2% + VAT commission) claims the VAT back, so for them the real cost is X.
- If the seller is an osek patur, there is no VAT to add; the agent cannot claim any back, so 300 ILS from an osek patur costs the agent the same as about 254 ILS + VAT from an osek murshe.
- Revenue check against the osek patur ceiling: 122,833 ILS a year is about 10,236 ILS a month, which is about 34 customers at 300 ILS, or 41 at 250 ILS. Past that, the seller must become an osek murshe.

### Gaps
- I did not open a gov.il page directly for the VAT rate; the confirmations are from a Deloitte tax alert, CPA guidance and press, all citing the Tax Authority.
- The consumer-protection rule on showing prices including VAT (for private customers) was not researched; it matters less here since buyers are businesses.
- The osek patur ceiling figure is from an accounting-software vendor, not from the Tax Authority site itself.

## 5. Typical income and commission of an Israeli real-estate agent

### Takeaway
Commission is usually 2% + VAT from each side, though not fixed by law. A typical agent earns from about 8,000-12,000 ILS a month when starting out to 20,000-40,000 ILS when active; CBS puts the average wage in "real estate activity" at 15,054 ILS (November 2024). One deal pays tens of thousands of shekels, so a 300 ILS/month tool is paid for by a small share of one deal.

### Cited Findings
- Bizportal (2026-09-08): standard commission 2% from one side (1-2% in weak markets). Monthly income: beginner (one deal per two months) 8,000-12,000 ILS or zero; intermediate (2 deals a month) 14,000-20,000; active (3-4 deals) 20,000-40,000; office trainee on base pay minimum wage to 8,000. Example: 2% of a 3M ILS flat = 60,000 ILS; of 1.2M = 24,000 ILS. Building a client base takes about two years; costs named include marketing, portal fees, office rent or commission split, car. [Bizportal](https://www.bizportal.co.il/career/news/article/20039772)
- Nadlan Center (2025-02-04), quoting the Central Bureau of Statistics: average wage in "real estate activity" (includes agents and brokers) was **15,054 ILS** in November (2024), up 8% from a year earlier; national average 13,068 ILS. This is employees' wage, so it may not reflect self-employed agents. [Nadlan Center](https://www.nadlancenter.co.il/article/11502)
- The usual commission when selling a flat is 2% + VAT from each side, but no law sets 2%; it is negotiable. (Search result summary.) [Elkayam Law](https://elkayamlaw.co.il/%D7%A2%D7%9E%D7%9C%D7%AA-%D7%AA%D7%99%D7%95%D7%95%D7%9A-%D7%9E%D7%9B%D7%99%D7%A8%D7%AA-%D7%93%D7%99%D7%A8%D7%94/%D7%A0%D7%93%D7%9C%D7%9F-%D7%95%D7%9E%D7%A7%D7%A8%D7%A7%D7%A2%D7%99%D7%9F/%D7%AA%D7%99%D7%95%D7%95%D7%9A/)

### Inferences
- At 300 ILS + VAT a month (3,600 ILS a year), the tool costs about 6% of one 60,000 ILS commission, or 15% of one 24,000 ILS commission, per year. If it brings one extra deal a year, it pays for itself many times over.
- For a beginner earning 8,000-12,000 ILS a month, 300 ILS is 2.5-4% of income: noticeable, so a free trial and no commitment (the market norm) matter.

### Gaps
- No official (CBS or Tax Authority) figure for self-employed agents' income; the CBS figure covers employees in the sector.
- No source on how many agents in Israel actively post listings to Facebook groups, or how much time they spend on it.
