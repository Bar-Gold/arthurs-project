## Which tools still post to Facebook groups in 2025-2026, and what do they charge per month?

### Takeaway
Every tool that still really posts to groups does it by driving the user's own logged-in browser (almost all are Chrome extensions). For about 80 groups, 1 account and 1-2 posts a day, prices run from about $6/month (yearly extension plans) to $47/month (PilotPoster); the middle of the market is $10-37/month.

### Cited Findings
- PilotPoster: one plan, $47/month or $390/year ($32.50/month), first month $24, unlimited groups, one licence per Facebook profile or Page, 7-day money-back, no free trial; Chrome extension, "posts publish through Chrome on your computer": [PilotPoster pricing](https://www.pilotposter.com/pricing/)
- Group Posting Pro: Lite free with 5 group-posts a day; Plus EUR 69/year only (EUR 5.75/month) removes the cap and adds once/daily/weekly schedules; Pro EUR 24/month (EUR 14 first month), EUR 39/quarter or EUR 119/year; Growth EUR 139/month done-for-you (14 posts, up to 30 groups); Agency from EUR 199/month; Chrome and Edge extension for desktop: [Group Posting Pro](https://groupposting.com/)
- MultiGroupPoster: free 6 posts / 6 groups; Pro $9.99/month or $69.99/year, unlimited posts and groups, profile plus managed Pages, priority email, 7-day money-back once per customer; "runs in your browser": [MultiGroupPoster](https://multiplegroupposter.com/facebook-auto-poster/)
- FB Group Bulk Poster: free 6 posts; Pro $9.99/month or $69.99/year ("42% savings"), unlimited posts and groups, scheduling, Spintax; no lifetime plan: [FB Group Bulk Poster pricing](https://fbgroupbulkposter.com/pricing)
- NinjaPoster: $37/month flat, no tiers, RSS-to-groups, AI spin, media library, 7-day refund: [NinjaPoster blog, 2026](https://ninjaposter.io/best-facebook-group-auto-posters/)
- GP Group Poster: lifetime $79 (listed regular $499), 1 Facebook and LinkedIn account, unlimited devices, lifetime updates, WhatsApp/Messenger support: [StackSocial deal](https://stacksocial.com/sales/gp-group-poster-lifetime-subscription)
- Facebook Group Poster (GoizanTools): Pro $69, Plus $109, Premium $139 per user per year; listed as Windows desktop: [GetApp](https://www.getapp.com/marketing-software/a/facebook-group-poster/), [Capterra](https://www.capterra.ca/software/1083944/Facebook-Group-Poster) (review sites only)
- Somiibo: Premium $15.95/month billed yearly, free version, up to 30 sessions: [Capterra](https://www.capterra.ca/software/169419/somiibo-social-media-bot) (review site only)
- A comparison article on fbgroupbulkposter.com (dated 2026-02-20) lists "Hootsuite (Groups Add-on) $99/month, up to 50 groups", "GroupPoster.io $39", "PostMate Pro $24", "Phantombuster $56", "ManageFlitter FB $12": [fbgroupbulkposter.com blog](https://fbgroupbulkposter.com/blog/best-facebook-auto-poster-tools-2026). Treat as unreliable: it is written by a competitor, and its Hootsuite claim contradicts the API removal below; none of these were checked on official pages.

### Inferences
- Cheapest real fit for the client's workload: Group Posting Pro Plus (EUR 69/year) or MultiGroupPoster / FB Group Bulk Poster yearly ($69.99/year), about $6/month. Monthly billing: $9.99 (MultiGroupPoster) to $47 (PilotPoster).
- Group limits are no longer a pricing lever: every paid plan found is "unlimited groups". Vendors price by account (licence per profile) and by features (AI, schedules, cloud backup).
- None of these is a stand-alone Windows desktop app driving the user's own Chrome; they are extensions living inside Chrome. That is the nearest model to the product being priced.

### Gaps
- FewFeed: no pricing page found.
- Somiibo and GoizanTools: price only from review sites; not confirmed on vendor pages, and whether Somiibo posts to groups was not confirmed.
- Video support per tool was not checked on each page.

## Are they subscription, lifetime licence, or per-post pricing?

### Takeaway
Almost all are subscriptions (monthly, with a yearly discount of about 30-42%). Lifetime licences appear only as deal-site offers. No per-post pricing found; free tiers are capped by posts instead.

### Cited Findings
- PilotPoster yearly saves $174 vs monthly ($390 vs $564): [PilotPoster pricing](https://www.pilotposter.com/pricing/)
- FB Group Bulk Poster yearly = "42% savings", and it states no lifetime plan: [FB Group Bulk Poster pricing](https://fbgroupbulkposter.com/pricing)
- Group Posting Pro sells monthly, quarterly and yearly, and its Plus plan only yearly: [Group Posting Pro](https://groupposting.com/)
- GP Group Poster lifetime $79, new users only, final once redeemed: [StackSocial](https://stacksocial.com/sales/gp-group-poster-lifetime-subscription)
- Free tiers are limited by posts: 6 posts once (MultiGroupPoster, FB Group Bulk Poster), 5 group-posts a day (Group Posting Pro Lite): [MultiGroupPoster](https://multiplegroupposter.com/facebook-auto-poster/), [Group Posting Pro](https://groupposting.com/)

- Refund windows are short: 7 days (PilotPoster, MultiGroupPoster, NinjaPoster), 14 days (Group Posting Pro Growth): sources above.

- Market default to compare against: about $10/month or about $70/year for a single account, unlimited groups.

## Did Meta's Groups API deprecation (2024) change the market?

### Takeaway
Yes. Meta announced on 2024-01-23 (Graph API v19.0) that the Groups API, including `publish_to_groups` and `groups_access_member_info`, would be removed from all versions in 90 days (removed 2024-04-22). Since then the API-based schedulers (Buffer, Publer, SocialPilot, Agorapulse, Zoho, Make) can only send a phone reminder; the only tools that still auto-post to groups are the ones driving the user's own browser.

### Cited Findings
- Meta: "The Facebook Groups API is deprecated in v19 and in 90 days will be removed from all versions", covering "all Permissions (publish_to_groups, groups_access_member_info) and Reviewable Features (Groups API)": [Meta for Developers, 2024-01-23](https://developers.facebook.com/blog/post/2024/01/23/introducing-facebook-graph-and-marketing-api-v19/)
- Removal date 2024-04-22: [Buffer help](https://support.buffer.com/articles/connecting-and-publishing-to-your-facebook-group-with-buffer-VevqG0mHZG); developer threads on it: [Meta developer community](https://developers.facebook.com/community/threads/2076398286093257/)
- Buffer: "It is no longer possible to schedule posts to be sent automatically due to changes Meta made to their Facebook Groups API in April 2024"; posting is now a phone notification and a manual paste: [Buffer help](https://support.buffer.com/articles/connecting-and-publishing-to-your-facebook-group-with-buffer-VevqG0mHZG)
- Publer: "Meta has recently removed public API access for Facebook Groups. We now only have access to the Pages API"; "posting in Groups now requires the use of reminders"; no insights for groups (article updated 20/10/2025): [Publer help](https://publer.com/help/en/article/how-to-connect-facebook-groups-1lrfn2h)
- SocialPilot: mobile reminders with "Share Now" in its app: [SocialPilot help](https://help.socialpilot.co/article/705-getting-started-with-socialpilot-mobile-app) (search snippet)
- Agorapulse, Zoho Social and Make announced dropping Facebook Groups: [Agorapulse](https://support.agorapulse.com/en/articles/9167708-facebook-groups-update-april-2024), [Zoho](https://help.zoho.com/portal/pt/community/topic/discontinuing-facebook-groups-due-to-api-deprecation?page=20), [Make](https://community.make.com/t/facebook-groups-will-be-discontinued-due-to-api-deprecation/32657) (search snippets)
- Meta Business Suite can still schedule to groups the user manages, which no third-party tool can since the deprecation: [PostPlanify blog](https://postplanify.com/blog/best-facebook-scheduling-tools) (third-party claim, not checked with Meta)
- Mainstream prices for context (from search snippets, not official pages): SocialPilot about $50/month for small teams; SocialBee $19 / $39 / $79 a month; Postoplan "Start" $1.90 and "Business" $6.90 a month (old figures, date unknown): [search summary](https://contentmavericks.com/best-facebook-groups-poster/), [Gappsy](https://www.gappsy.com/tools/postoplan/)

### Inferences
- The market split in two after April 2024: API schedulers ($6-100+/month) that only remind, and browser-driving group posters ($6-47/month) that actually post. A real-estate agent who wants 40-80 groups posted without touching the phone can only use the second kind.
- Meta Business Suite only helps for groups the user administers; agents post to groups run by others, so it does not cover this case (inferred, not confirmed by Meta).

### Gaps
- Hootsuite: no official statement found on its groups support after April 2024.
- Postoplan: official site returned HTTP 522 twice; its current prices and groups support are not confirmed. The $1.90 / $6.90 figures are from an aggregator and may be old.
- Official pricing pages for Buffer, Hootsuite, SocialPilot and Publer were not read (out of call budget); they matter less since none auto-posts to groups.
